**Title:** Consistent Hashing — Rebalance nodes without mass data reshuffle

### Replication
**Title:** Replication — Use leader-follower or multi-leader models for availability  
**Summary:**  
Replication involves maintaining multiple copies of data across different nodes to enhance system availability and fault tolerance. The article describes two primary replication models: leader-follower and multi-leader. In the leader-follower model, one node acts as the leader, handling all writes, while followers replicate data and serve read requests. In the multi-leader model, multiple nodes can accept writes, improving write scalability but introducing challenges around consistency. The article discusses the trade-offs between these models, including consistency, latency, and complexity, and provides guidance on choosing the right model for specific use cases.
